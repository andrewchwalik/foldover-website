A little help, when you need it

We’re here to help.

Questions, feedback, or something not working as expected? Email support@foldover.xyz.

Tell us what happened

Include your device, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please don’t send passwords or sensitive task information.

Getting the app

Foldover’s public App Store release is being prepared. Contact support to ask about early access. Existing users can open the web app.

Where did my unfinished tasks go?

Unfinished tasks carry forward into the next day. Previous days keep a read-only record, with completed tasks first and unfinished tasks below.

Account and privacy requests

For help with your account, your data, or an account deletion request, contact support@foldover.xyz.
